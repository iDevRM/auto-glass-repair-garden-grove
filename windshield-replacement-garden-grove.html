What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

The job starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to a thin, even layer. Any bare metal on the frame gets primed so rust cannot start under the new seal, which still matters this close to the salty coastal air. Then fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Plan on about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, common for 405 and 22 commuters, add up to weak glass.

A leak after the first rain

Water inside after a winter storm often points to a failed seal.

Pitting that glares at sunset

Years of grit leave tiny pits that make the low sun and headlights flare.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in Southern California Heat?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a rock chip breaks the outer layer. Glass expands as it heats and shrinks as it cools, and every swing pulls at the ends of the break. In Garden Grove the worst moment is a car that sat in a sunny parking lot all afternoon, then gets the air conditioning cranked to full the second it starts. During a fall heat wave with Santa Ana winds, the glass can swing a long way in a few minutes. A chip picked up in spring is often a crack across the glass by the end of summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, and warm weather keeps it toward the short end. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass comes from the same supplier that made your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed.

Can I use the air conditioning right after a new windshield?

Yes, once the technician says the car is ready to drive. For the first few days, start it on a gentle setting instead of blasting cold air at hot glass.
